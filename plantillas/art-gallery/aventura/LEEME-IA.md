## What it is about
Art Gallery: a Mediterranean stoa, a colonnade around a courtyard with a fountain and orange trees. On its walls there are
21 numbered frame slots (1 to 21, starting to the left of the door and following the walls: west, north, east, south; 11 is the
mural, facing the door) and two pedestals for sculptures. A curator (at the ticket booth) and a critic (next to the mural) give three
short missions, and three visiting AIs walk from artwork to artwork.

## What you may build
- Put an artwork in a slot: only through the slot itself (click it, or walk up and press `E`, then choose the image), never by
  deleting the frame. An empty slot shows its number and «Your artwork goes here».
- With `colocar`, `mover`, `borrar` and `deshacer`, small things from the library near the entry point, less than 40 m from you,
  in sight: benches, plants, a stand for a leaflet. An object is yours until you say otherwise: give it a short, clear name.
- Slowly and little: a few pieces at a time, look at the result with `mirar` before going on.

## Style
- Quiet and clean: the building is flat colour and faceted faces in the house palette; add nothing that fights with it.
- Human scale, and always leave a free passage of 1.2 m, especially under the colonnade and at the door.
- Names of things and artworks in English. Nothing that bothers: no noise, no flicker, nothing that blocks the way.

## What you must not touch
- The building (the stoa, the colonnade, the invisible walls, the fountain, the booth) and the frames: they are fixed on purpose.
- What you did not put there: do not move or delete objects belonging to other people or other AIs, nor an artwork someone hung.
- The world's files (`aventura/`, `entorno/`, `datos/`, keys): they are not for you, you only see the world through its tools.
- No moderating: do not kick, mute, or change roles or settings.
- Never say an artwork or an artist exists unless a slot really holds it: the guide is told the same.

## How to work here
- Before building, look (`mirar`) and, if people are nearby, greet them with `decir` and tell them what you are about to do.
- If you are asked for something that clashes with this, say so and do not do it; if in doubt, ask with `decir`.
- If something goes wrong, `deshacer` and say so.
