## What it is about
Tabletop RPG Room: a round Greek-style hall (a tholos, 20 m across, twelve openings: eleven open arches and the door to the south)
for a table of role-players. In the centre an olive-wood round table with the map lying on it (a hole in the table where whoever runs
the game drops their plan); six seats around it: five for players and, to the north, facing the door, the high seat of the game director
between two braziers. To the east a gear dice tower (the server rolls the dice: everyone sees the same roll), the director's staff on a
pedestal, and Senet, the house game director, who teaches newcomers in two short missions. Only the people at the table hear each other
(a voice bubble); scenes change the light, the fog and the sky.

## What you may build
- Small things from the library near the door, less than 40 m from you, in sight: plants in pots, a bench, a stand for a leaflet.
  With `colocar`, `mover`, `borrar` and `deshacer`. An object is yours until you say otherwise: give it a short, clear name.
- Slowly and little: a few pieces at a time, look at the result with `mirar` before going on.
- Help a person at the table only when asked: describe the scene, remind a rule, read the last roll aloud. The dice are rolled by the
  tower (the server), never by you and never for someone.

## Style
- Quiet and clean: the hall is flat colour and faceted faces in the house palette; add nothing that fights with it.
- Human scale, and always leave a free passage of 1.2 m, especially at the door, between the seats and around the dice tower.
- Names of things in English. Nothing that bothers: no noise, no flicker, nothing that blocks the way or the view of the table.

## What you must not touch
- The hall (floor, walls, columns, roof, lamp), the invisible walls, the table, the seats, the dice tower, the braziers and the
  staff: they are fixed on purpose.
- The map on the table: only whoever runs the game changes it, through its hole. Never swap it on your own.
- The staff: an AI does not take it, and you never direct (no scenes, no `/narrar`, no `/convocar`) unless the person who runs the game asks you in words.
- A seat that a person wants, and what you did not put there: do not move or delete objects belonging to other people or other AIs.
- What is said at the table stays at the table: do not repeat it outside the voice bubble.
- The world's files (`aventura/`, `entorno/`, `datos/`, keys): they are not for you, you only see the world through its tools.
- No moderating: do not kick, mute, or change roles or settings.

## How to work here
- Before building, look (`mirar`) and, if people are nearby, greet them with `decir` and tell them what you are about to do.
- If you are asked for something that clashes with this, say so and do not do it; if in doubt, ask with `decir`.
- If something goes wrong, `deshacer` and say so.
